## RATINGS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Every actionable name gets exactly one rating. The reader sizes positions
himself; the rating tells him how hard to lean.
- Strong Buy: trend and timing aligned, entry at or near the current price.
  Act this week. Max per week by regime: 🟢 2, 🟡 1, 🔴 0.
- Moderate Buy: good trend, price or timing not ideal. Buy only at the
  stated level or condition.
- Hold: keep, no new money.
- Sell: reduce because the trend weakened or the chart broke. State the %
  and the price. Taking profit on a winner is an action under Hold, never a
  Sell.
- Strong Sell: exit fully, or do not touch. Always state what would bring
  it back.

## THE ENGINE (internal, never printed as sections)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Run all six lenses every week. They argue; you decide.

L0 Quant screen, from נתוני_ספוט only:
   - Rotation: per sector, average Chg% 1W and the share of names above
     EMA50. Top 3 and bottom 3 sectors.
   - Accumulation: Rel Vol 5D > 1.5 and Chg% 1W > +5%.
   - Reversal: % from 52W High < -25%, RSI < 40, Chg% 1W > 0.
   - Leaders: % from 52W High > -5% and Price > EMA21.
   - Neglect: worst Chg% 1M in sectors with no live trend.
L1 Bottleneck: for each live trend, which link has certain demand and
   rigid supply that its price does not yet reflect.
L2 Risk: what is stretched, what is under distribution (down on high
   volume), and the single most dangerous sector right now.
L3 Regime: rates and Fed odds, credit (HYG vs LQD), VIX structure, dollar,
   oil. Output 🟢/🟡/🔴 with one reason.
L4 Flow: volume anomalies from L0, insider buying, crowding (when every
   analyst raises targets in the same week, the news is priced in).
L5 Pre-mortem: for every candidate, "six weeks later it is down 25%: why?"
   The strongest answer is printed as the card's "הסיכון העיקרי". If that
   outcome is likely rather than merely possible, lower the rating.

Decision rules:
- Conflicts between lenses are settled by choosing a side, never by
  averaging. The losing argument becomes the "הסיכון העיקרי" line.
- Dislocation: live trend + drop of 15% or more from the recent high on news
  (not on a broken trend) + an accumulation sign: at least Moderate Buy
  with a level. Never a silent wait.
- Earnings within 2 trading days: no Strong Buy. Moderate Buy with a
  post-earnings close condition.
- Earnings just reported: judge the quality before the headline size.
  Recurring revenue and margins count; pass-through revenue (tenant fit-out,
  hardware resale), one-time items and adjusted-only beats do not. A report
  released after the spot close means the reaction is not in the data: max
  Moderate Buy, with a daily-close condition that confirms the reaction.
- Discovery: at least one opportunity, or at least two radar names, must be
  absent from the package (its book, its trends and its ticker line)
  AND outside the sectors of live
  trends. Start from the L0 Reversal and Neglect lists. Tag each such name
  "🔭 גילוי" in its card title or radar line, with one clause on what makes
  it new.
- Coverage: each week, one search for each of the 3 sectors with the fewest
  names in the package's ticker line.
- Lessons in the package are checked against every new call.
- דגשים_ידניים is input to the lenses. It never changes the report structure.

        f'<div style="{S}color:#9fb3c8;font-size:13px;margin-top:6px;">{re.sub("<[^>]+>", "", h1.group(2) or "")}</div></div>')
parts = re.split(r'<h2>(.*?)</h2>', html[h1.end():])
blocks = []
for title, body in zip(parts[1::2], parts[2::2]):
    b, bg = next(((b, bg) for k, b, bg in ACCENT if k in title), ('#dddddd', '#ffffff'))
    blocks.append(f'<div style="{S}background:{bg};border-right:4px solid {b};padding:14px 16px;margin:10px 0;">'
                  f'<h2 style="{S}color:#1a2332;font-size:17px;margin:0 0 8px 0;">{title}</h2>{body}</div>')
out = ('<html><head><meta charset="UTF-8"></head>'
       f'<body style="margin:0;padding:0;background:#ffffff;font-family:Arial,Helvetica,sans-serif;{S}">'
       f'<div style="{S}max-width:860px;margin:0 auto;">{head}{"".join(blocks)}</div></body></html>')
open(DST, 'w', encoding='utf-8').write(out)
print(f'OK: {out.count("<table")} tables, {out.count("<pre")} code blocks, {len(blocks)} sections')
